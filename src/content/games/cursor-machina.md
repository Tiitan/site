## Overview

Cursor Machina is a 3D Runner/Platformer video game for PC, Mac and Linux. You control a robot with fantastic physical abilities and the power to rewind time, and you have to reach the end of every levels.

Find the fastest route to raise your name in the leaderboards, watch replays or other players, or just explore the levels and all the various ways to end them at your own pace.

## Features

- 10 timed maps
- Online leaderboard
- Deterministic time rewind
- control mechanics: Wall jump, dash slide, Deterministic time rewind
- Parkour elements: bumpers, sliding surfaces, sticky surfaces
- Portal-like teleporters
