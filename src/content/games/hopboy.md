## Overview

Hop ! Hop ! Hop !

Help "Hop Boy" climb as high as possible.
Touch anywhere on the screen to make him jump, reach the top, and avoid obstacles.

The faster you tap, the faster Hop Boy climbs.
Collect gems on the way to unlock new characters.

This game is intentionally hard.
Are you ready to reach at least 100 points?

## Features

- Manage your timing to climb as high as possible
- Avoid fixed and moving obstacles
- 10 languages available
- Cartoon visual style
- Around ten unlockable characters
- Score tracking and leaderboard
