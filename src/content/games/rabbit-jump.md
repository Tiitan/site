## Overview

Rabbit jump is a Mario-like platformer prototype built around short levels packed with hazards such as moving platforms and spikes.

The player controls a continuously jumping rabbit using phone gyroscope input. Collect 3 carrots in each level to clear it and move to the next challenge.
